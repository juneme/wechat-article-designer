## Design fingerprint

- **Visual thesis**: bind color into a sequence of cover, index, plate, gatefold, bookmark, and sleeve.
- **Palette**: paper white and graphite with gray cobalt, madder rose, sage, and a small mustard correction.
- **Opening**: use a high-color cover with an inset paper label and short color registration strip.
- **Section rhythm**: move from fold-tab index to circular lead plate, four-edge mount, gatefold, bookmark detail, and closed sleeve.
- **Image behavior**: assign a circle to the core subject, a mounted panorama to the setting, and a capsule to a detail.
- **Closing**: close the article inside an archive sleeve rather than leaving an open ruled ending.

## Reuse boundary

Use the route when a literal bound-folio metaphor fits the subject. Do not use its cover-plus-tabs skeleton as the default answer for colorful artbook work. For briefs prioritizing artistry, harmony, tactile surprise, or a poetic gallery rhythm, start from `paper-cut-artbook.md` and rebuild the whole-page composition.

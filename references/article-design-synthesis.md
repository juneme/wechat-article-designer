# Article Design Synthesis

Use this reference to turn one article's meaning into an original visual system. The output is the composition itself, not a form, score, or machine design contract.

## Start with the article's own material

Extract visual forces from the content:

- concrete objects, places, seasons, eras, tools, surfaces, and image evidence;
- scale, repetition, sequence, accumulation, comparison, interruption, and release;
- the emotional trajectory from opening to close;
- the difference between verified information, interpretation, atmosphere, and action;
- the reader's likely pace, attention, and reason for continuing.

Convert those forces into a visual thesis. A visual thesis is a relationship such as “the article opens compressed and gradually acquires air” or “each evidence section behaves like a field note around one recurring red line.” It is not a style label.

## Explore before committing

When the article has several plausible visual readings, sketch two or three directions privately. Keep each sketch light: opening move, type character, dominant field, media rhythm, motif, and one signature transition. Choose the direction that best clarifies the article and creates a memorable reading experience.

Do not ask the user to approve these working directions unless they explicitly requested options. Implement the strongest candidate and let the finished draft carry the decision.

## Compose relationships, not components

Decide how these relationships work together:

| Layer | Question |
|---|---|
| Reading order | Where does the eye enter, pause, turn, and leave? |
| Density | Which passages compress, which open, and where is the visual peak? |
| Type | What changes in scale, weight, alignment, family, or orientation express hierarchy? |
| Color | Which colors establish field, ink, signal, transition, and atmosphere? |
| Media | Does each image prove, locate, explain, compare, immerse, or pace? |
| Geometry | Which edge, stroke, frame, crop, or path belongs to the subject? |
| Motion | What becomes clearer or more felt through sequence, reveal, drift, or transformation? |
| Closing | Does the article resolve, invite action, leave an image, or return to its opening motif? |

No layer is mandatory as decoration. No number of colors, cards, SVG scenes, type sizes, or spacing values is inherently correct. Repetition may establish identity; irregularity may carry narrative energy. Judge the whole composition.

## Keep evidence and atmosphere distinct

Generated imagery can set mood, illustrate a concept, or build a narrative scene. It cannot stand in for a real certificate, official seal, event photograph, person, product result, or institutional proof. Keep qualifiers next to the claims they limit. Make an action, deadline, source, or safety condition readable without animation.

## Use the full expressive surface

The WeChat fragment may combine open prose, typographic posters, full-width media, narrow marginal notes, dark and light fields, borders, shadows, gradients, masks, irregular section rhythm, and SVG/SMIL scenes. Techniques are available together; there is no capability tier.

Compatibility affects implementation and testing, not the ambition of the concept. When a fragile effect has a readable initial state and a reasonable editor fallback, keep the idea and inspect the actual draft. Do not redesign the whole article into a conservative template merely because one declaration produces a warning.

## Optional post-composition observation

After the article is complete, `article_workspace.py inspect` may record counts of SVG scenes, motion nodes, colors, and font sizes. This report is descriptive and advisory. It cannot reject a design, require markers, or become a target for future composition.

## Final synthesis review

- The article's structure and visual rhythm express the same argument.
- The opening has a distinctive reason to look the way it does.
- Repeated motifs evolve or accumulate meaning instead of becoming wallpaper.
- Strong moments have enough quiet space around them.
- Visual reference material has been translated through the article rather than copied as a template.
- The ending performs a reader job and does not merely stop after the last module.
- Removing optional motion leaves the reading order and essential meaning intact.
- No hidden machine convention has flattened intentional variation.

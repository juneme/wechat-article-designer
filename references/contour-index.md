# Contour Field Notes

Use this route for an artbook-family composition built from topographic notation, specimen indexing, stepped reading levels, and quiet scientific observation.

## Evidence boundary

**Compatibility status**: passed WeChat-path compatibility, Creative audit, and 320px, 375px, and 390px source/fallback browser checks. Editor version, device, pasted DOM, and property-by-property preservation are unknown.

**Originality status**: whole-page silhouette remains too close to Paper Cut Artbook. Store the fragment as compatible design DNA and an artbook subfamily, not as evidence that local component changes automatically create a distinct gestalt.

## Design fingerprint

- **Visual thesis**: turn attention hierarchy into a calm topographic survey.
- **Palette**: fog white and graphite reading field, mineral teal primary family, steel blue secondary family, and a small vermilion correction.
- **Opening**: pair a compact title field with a circular altitude or contour mark.
- **Section rhythm**: use progressively inset rules and rows to express reading height.
- **Image behavior**: use one organic lead window, one circular core specimen, and a quieter horizontal sample drawer.
- **Recurring motif**: altitude values, contour rings, transects, field labels, specimen codes, and survey closure.
- **Closing**: resolve the page as a completed survey rather than a generic CTA.

## Structural grammar

1. Use stepped margins for no more than three short levels; keep the narrowest row readable at 320px.
2. Keep repeating radial fields decorative or paired with independently readable text and a solid background fallback.
3. Use organic and circular image silhouettes only for distinct narrative roles.
4. Keep the sample drawer as a manual `overflow-x:auto` strip with stable order and a static swipe cue.
5. Preserve ordinary source order so the conservative fallback can expand to a single column.

## Reuse boundary

Do not combine a white field, large left-aligned headline, oversized organic lead image, open editorial chapters, rounded secondary image, swipe strip, and airy ending when the request asks for a new direction after Paper Cut. Change the whole-page massing or explicitly describe the result as a Contour variation within the artbook family.

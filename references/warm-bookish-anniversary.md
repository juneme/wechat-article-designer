# Warm Bookish Anniversary Pattern

Use this direction for anniversaries, cultural programs, reading communities, education, reflective event narratives, and institutional stories that should feel warm without looking ceremonial or nostalgic.

Start from `assets/warm-bookish-anniversary-article.html`. Replace every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}`, remove unused optional blocks, and preserve the template's flat single-column structure. Never copy project names, dates, photo filenames, participant counts, or claims from a prior article.

## Visual Contract

- Use a warm paper-white reading surface, one brick or muted-red primary, one bronze or olive-brown secondary, and dark brown body text.
- Keep the layout flat and editorial: thin rules, compact numbered chapter markers, small bilingual labels when useful, and square or lightly rounded image frames.
- Avoid seals, faux certificates, imitation newspaper mastheads, gold gradients, calligraphy effects, and ornamental nostalgia unless the supplied brand system explicitly requires them.
- Keep all top-level blocks on the same horizontal baseline and let vertical spacing create rhythm.

## Article Sequence

1. Open with a short top rule, main-visual placeholder, literal title, date or edition marker, and one concise positioning sentence.
2. Divide the narrative into three to five chapters. Give each chapter a stable number, a short action word, and a concrete Chinese heading.
3. Alternate short copy blocks with one strong photo or one small photo group. Insert a divider after two or three consecutive images.
4. Use a restrained solid-color quote or takeaway strip for the core sentence; do not wrap every paragraph in a card.
5. Close with a factual next step, signature, or commemorative sentence. Keep the closing compact and avoid a heavy dark panel unless requested.

## Photo Treatment

- Use square or 4-8px-radius frames with a warm-neutral placeholder surface.
- Keep captions factual and short. Do not embed project-specific filenames in reusable markup.
- For large participant sets, prefer one finished collage bitmap or verified single-column cards over a very long horizontal strip.

## Template Editing Rules

- Keep the opening, three numbered chapters, one takeaway strip, and compact closing as the default narrative spine.
- Duplicate a chapter or photo frame only when the supplied copy requires it; renumber all duplicated labels consistently.
- Replace every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` before delivery. Do not leave generic template copy in the published fragment.
- Keep the source as a bare WeChat fragment. Create a separate UTF-8 preview host only for browser review.
- Do not reintroduce the original 42-item horizontal gallery. Use a finished collage bitmap or verified single-column repeated cards for large groups.

## Validation

Run the standard article audit in `SKILL.md`. Also confirm that the result contains no copied brand names or event facts from examples, headings remain readable at 320px, and the warm palette does not reduce body-text contrast below 4.5:1.

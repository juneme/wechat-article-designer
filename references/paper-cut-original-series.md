## Botanical Press

- **Thesis**: preserve a period of growth as a specimen held between pages.
- **Palette**: paper gray, forest green, pale lavender, mist blue, and a small clay-red annotation.
- **Sequence**: specimen spine → leaf-cut lead plate → three-distance observation ledger → tissue interleaf → circular close study → seed envelope.
- **Image roles**: asymmetric leaf plate for the subject; circle for texture or gesture.
- **Avoid**: generic botanical decoration, repeated green cards, or a second arch-led Paper Cut opener.

## Cut-Paper Atlas

- **Thesis**: let cut shapes determine where color and reading direction live.
- **Palette**: vermilion, dusty blue, sage, pale lavender, paper white, and a small yellow register.
- **Sequence**: split-color cover → nested large/small color field → negative image window → unequal registration swatches → three-layer stacked index.
- **Image roles**: one clipped environment or portrait window; color swatches remain non-image rhythm markers.
- **Avoid**: equal card grids, four equally saturated large fields, or treating palette changes as structural originality.

## Conservation Folio

- **Thesis**: show original material, loss, and repair as an honest relationship.
- **Palette**: old paper, graphite, faded blue, repair red, and restrained mustard.
- **Sequence**: accession cover → damaged-edge condition plate → stepped repair log → paired state board → conservator strip → archive box.
- **Image roles**: irregular condition plate for the main evidence; paired rectangles only for compact before/after states.
- **Avoid**: fake certificates, official seals, nostalgic museum ornament, or using damage shapes as decoration without narrative purpose.

## Poetic Zine

- **Thesis**: make pauses, unfinished sentences, and one dense interruption carry the page.
- **Palette**: white, mist blue, pale lavender, coral, near-black, and a tiny yellow correction.
- **Sequence**: open white title page → dome lead image → extreme quiet page → folded line → one dense black page → partial detail image → three-layer last fold.
- **Image roles**: calm dome for the opener; one partial-corner detail for the close view.
- **Avoid**: filling the white page with ornaments, repeating the black band, or turning every sentence into a quote card.

## Material Board

- **Thesis**: let surface, weight, scale, and joints determine editorial hierarchy.
- **Palette**: concrete gray, clay red, stone green, glass blue, graphite, and a small construction yellow.
- **Sequence**: material-strip cover → unequal-width weight ledger → black-mounted panorama → stepped scale samples → joint detail → bound edge.
- **Image roles**: hard panoramic space plate for context; pentagonal joint detail for the final close view.
- **Avoid**: dashboard grids, equal sample tiles, decorative construction motifs, or warm lifestyle rounding.
